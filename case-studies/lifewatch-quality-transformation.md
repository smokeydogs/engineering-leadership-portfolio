# Building a scalable quality operating system

**Organization:** LifeWatch  
**Role:** Director of Quality Assurance  
**Period:** April 2016–April 2017  
**Domain:** Healthcare technology and mobile cardiac monitoring

## Context

LifeWatch operated in a healthcare environment where connected monitoring products, mobile workflows, clinical operations, and customer support required dependable software and disciplined release practices.

## Leadership challenge

Quality signals and delivery practices needed to scale across onshore and offshore teams while improving coordination among Product, Development, Support, Operations, and executive leadership.

## My contribution

- Led healthcare technology quality strategy and established a scalable onshore and offshore delivery model.
- Reduced customer issues by more than 40% within 12 months by establishing quality KPIs and coordinating improvements across technical, support, and leadership teams.
- Standardized test management and release reporting through qTest.
- Expanded Selenium and Appium automation across web and mobile applications.
- Aligned Product, Development, Support, and Operations around roadmaps, release readiness, and customer priorities.

## Outcome

The organization gained clearer release visibility, more repeatable coverage, and a shared view of customer-impacting quality. The result was a substantial reduction in reported customer issues over the year.

## Leadership interpretation

In healthcare technology, quality requires both technical controls and organizational alignment. A useful quality operating system connects automation, release evidence, support signals, and executive decisions instead of treating testing as an isolated function.
