# Claim and sourcing methodology

This portfolio separates three kinds of information:

## 1. Experience statements

Role scope, dates, team responsibilities, technologies used in my work, and outcome metrics come from my approved résumé. They are written as first-person retrospective accounts rather than claims about a company's complete internal environment.

## 2. Public product context

Company and product descriptions are supported by publicly accessible company pages, press releases, support material, or product documentation. Public sources help a reader understand the market and user problem; they do not establish the private architecture of the systems I worked on.

## 3. Retrospective interpretation

Sections labeled “Leadership interpretation” describe what I learned and how I would explain the work today. They are not contemporaneous design documents.

## Confidentiality boundaries

This repository intentionally excludes:

- Employer-owned source code or reconstructed proprietary code
- Internal architecture diagrams, roadmaps, or security details
- Customer, employee, or partner data
- Credentials, configuration, or operational access information
- Private documents, screenshots, tickets, or pull-request content
- Claims that cannot be supported by the résumé or a public source

The architecture sketches are conceptual leadership models, not depictions of a former employer's production topology.
