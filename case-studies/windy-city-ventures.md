# Connecting operations, technology, and growth

**Organization:** Windy City Ventures  
**Role:** Vice President of Operations  
**Period:** May 2019–March 2022  
**Domain:** Concert-venue and hospitality operations

## Context

This operating role combined business development, technology operations, vendor economics, team development, digital acquisition, and the creation of new hospitality capabilities.

## My contribution

- Owned operational strategy, growth planning, technology operations, and process improvement.
- Increased sales by 30% through employee training and search-engine optimization initiatives.
- Reduced annual server-infrastructure costs by $150,000 through vendor negotiations.
- Launched a cloud-kitchen operation with digital ordering workflows, creating a delivery channel for multiple food brands.
- Led kitchen development for a new concert venue, coordinating site-selection input, permitting, and infrastructure buildout.

## Outcome

The work improved sales, lowered recurring technology costs, strengthened employee capability, and expanded the venue's food-service model with digitally enabled offerings.

## Leadership interpretation

Operating outside a traditional software company reinforced an important principle: technology leadership is valuable when it improves the whole business system. Vendor decisions, employee workflows, digital acquisition, physical operations, and customer experience often matter as much as application architecture.

*This case study is based on résumé-supported experience and intentionally excludes private business records or venue-specific operating details.*
