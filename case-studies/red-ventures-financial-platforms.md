# Modernizing high-traffic financial experiences

**Organization:** Red Ventures  
**Role:** Director of Engineering and Quality Automation  
**Period:** April 2017–November 2019  
**Products:** Bankrate.com, CreditCards.com, and related consumer platforms

## Context

Bankrate and CreditCards.com help consumers understand and compare financial products through content, data, interactive tools, and marketplace experiences. Red Ventures publicly describes its business as combining digital consumer experiences with analytics, data science, and integrated technology.

## Leadership challenge

The portfolio needed faster, safer delivery across high-traffic consumer web and mobile experiences where performance, conversion, accuracy, security, and release quality directly affected customers and commercial results.

## My contribution

- Directed engineering, quality automation, and application-security work across Bankrate.com, CreditCards.com, and related properties.
- Led the mobile-first redesign of CreditCards.com, contributing to a 35% year-over-year increase in credit-card sales.
- Increased AWS deployment throughput by 200% through containerized CI/CD using Docker and Kubernetes.
- Reduced production defects by 40% through automated web, API, and mobile testing.
- Established the first cross-functional Agile team in the organization, increasing sprint velocity by 30% and expanding delivery capacity.

## Technical approach

The work combined delivery-pipeline modernization with earlier, broader quality feedback. Automated validation across customer journeys, APIs, and mobile experiences helped shorten release cycles while improving confidence. Containerization and clearer team ownership reduced friction between building, testing, securing, and deploying software.

## Outcome

The transformation increased release throughput, reduced production defects, improved sprint flow, and supported measurable mobile conversion growth.

## Leadership interpretation

Quality automation has the most value when it changes delivery economics. The important outcome was not test volume; it was enabling teams to release more frequently with less production risk while staying focused on customer conversion and trust.
