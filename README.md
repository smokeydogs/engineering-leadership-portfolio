## Leadership system

Across these roles, the recurring system has been consistent:

1. Connect product and technology priorities to measurable customer and business outcomes.
2. Build leaders and teams with clear ownership boundaries and shared accountability.
3. Improve flow through better architecture, automation, quality, and operating cadence.
4. Treat reliability, security, accessibility, and observability as product capabilities.
5. Stay technically engaged where leadership judgment can unblock delivery or reduce risk.

See [how I lead](operating-model/how-i-lead.md) for the operating principles behind the case studies.

## About this repository

Most of the work described here was completed in private corporate repositories that I do not own and cannot reproduce. These retrospective case studies document my responsibilities and outcomes without publishing employer-confidential source code, internal architecture, customer information, credentials, or proprietary documents.

Public links provide independent product and company context; they are not presented as proof of private implementation details. See [claim and sourcing methodology](methodology/claim-sourcing.md).
