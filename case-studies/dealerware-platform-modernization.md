# Scaling an automotive mobility platform

**Organization:** Dealerware  
**Role:** Vice President of Software Development and Quality  
**Period:** March 2022–present  
**Domain:** Fleet-management and mobility SaaS for automotive retailers and OEMs

## Context

Dealerware operates a cloud-based fleet-management platform spanning web and mobile workflows, open APIs, reporting, contracts, vehicle programs, and integrations. Its public materials describe a unified mobility platform serving dealership and OEM programs across the United States and Canada.

## Leadership challenge

The central challenge was to scale a growing SaaS platform and its engineering organization while improving reliability, delivery flow, product alignment, and partner interoperability. That required modernization without losing focus on dealership operations or customer commitments.

## My contribution

- Led global software development, quality engineering, program management, and technology operations across domain teams.
- Helped scale the platform from approximately 1,200 to more than 3,500 dealerships and support more than 100,000 vehicles.
- Sustained 99.99% platform uptime while engineering throughput grew approximately 200% and Tier 2 escalations fell by more than 40%.
- Directed modernization work involving React, Node.js, Ruby, GraphQL, Flutter, and domain-driven design.
- Oversaw Product and Customer Success for eight months during a CPO search, aligning roadmap priorities with customer needs, commercial commitments, and engineering capacity.
- Established AI-enabled development workflows, an internal knowledge capability, customer-facing conversational workflows, and internal MCP integrations.
- Expanded adoption analytics and platform observability using ThoughtSpot Signal, Amplitude, and Datadog.
- Contributed technical judgment to acquisition due diligence across architecture, security, scalability, and integration complexity.

## Outcome

The work supported a period of platform and commercial growth, including 25% annual revenue growth and annual churn below 1%, while maintaining a strong reliability baseline.

## Leadership interpretation

Platform scale is as much an organizational problem as a software problem. Clear domains, better production feedback, consistent operating mechanisms, and leaders who own outcomes make modernization durable. Staying technically close helped connect architectural choices to delivery risk, customer experience, and investment decisions.
