# Quality engineering for retail analytics

**Organization:** Market6  
**Role:** Director of Quality Assurance  
**Period:** March 2015–April 2016  
**Domain:** Enterprise retail analytics and product-movement data

## Context

Market6 provided web-based retail collaboration and analytics capabilities for retailers and consumer-goods suppliers. In this environment, software quality depended on both the user-facing application and the accuracy, timeliness, security, and performance of the underlying data pipelines.

## Leadership challenge

The organization needed a quality model that addressed browser workflows, service behavior, high-volume analytical data, ETL correctness, and non-functional risk rather than relying primarily on manual functional testing.

## My contribution

- Owned quality engineering for enterprise big-data analytics products serving Kroger and other enterprise customers.
- Established an automated testing framework using MSTest, Selenium WebDriver, and SpecFlow.
- Defined quality controls for SQL and Netezza ETL pipelines, validating data accuracy and performance.
- Introduced quality reporting through Jira, Confluence, and Splunk.
- Established performance and security testing for enterprise analytics platforms.

## Outcome

The quality model accelerated test cycles, improved defect detection, increased release-risk visibility, and extended coverage into the data and non-functional layers most critical to an analytics product.

## Leadership interpretation

Analytics quality is inseparable from data quality. A polished interface cannot compensate for stale, incomplete, or incorrectly transformed information. Effective quality leadership follows the product's value stream from ingestion and transformation through analysis and user decisions.
